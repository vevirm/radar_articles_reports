# Matrix criteria audit — V17.13.26

This audit reviews all 16 cells for two opposite failure modes:

1. **under-reasoning** — a cell is defined so literally that a valid causal mechanism is never searched/classified; and
2. **over-reasoning** — generic words or an inferred bridge place a source in a cell without source evidence for the row and both Matrix axes.

The Matrix remains a 4×4 map of (a) European control/dependence and (b) R&I/competitive performance. Coverage is diagnostic, never a quota.

## People & knowledge

- **K-A — Build and retain talent.** Keep: European retention, returning researchers, research-career capacity and domestic/European research-workforce strengthening. Exclude from A: a specifically international/third-country talent inflow; that is K-C when it improves capacity but remains externally sourced.
- **K-B — Security slows exchange.** Require an EU/member-state safeguard (research/knowledge security, screening, protection) **and** a source-supported collaboration, mobility, openness, delay or burden cost. A security incident by itself is not evidence that safeguards impose the trade-off.
- **K-C — Capability from outside.** Include international/third-country researchers, postdocs, doctoral talent, STEM graduates, visiting researchers, foreign expertise and external research networks when the source links them to European R&I capacity/performance and continued access/retention is externally sourced.
- **K-D — People or knowledge are lost.** Broaden beyond brain drain to source-supported loss of research-network access, knowledge leakage/theft, exclusion or loss of know-how where the consequence is a European research/capability loss. Do not treat a generic foreign-interference mention as D without a knowledge/capability consequence.

## Tools & infrastructure

- **I-A — Resilient European capacity.** Include building/operating European capacity **and** diversification, alternative suppliers, joint procurement/shared facilities or other mechanisms that measurably reduce a critical dependency while sustaining capability. Literal ownership is not required.
- **I-B — More control, higher cost.** Include redundancy, localisation, trusted-supplier or sovereign-infrastructure choices only when the same evidence shows cost, delay, duplication or performance friction.
- **I-C — Frontier access from outside.** Include foreign/non-EU compute, cloud, instruments, facilities, suppliers or critical inputs only when the source says they actually enable European research/innovation performance **and** Europe remains dependent on access. Merely mentioning a foreign capability plus the word “capacity” is insufficient.
- **I-D — External dependence constrains capability.** Require a concrete restriction/chokepoint/shortage/access problem with a European R&I consequence. Generic “risk” or “vulnerability” language alone does not fill D.

## Firms & scale

- **C-A — European firms capture scale.** Require a European firm/production/commercialisation/industrial scaling mechanism. A generic EU fund or policy announcement belongs in Rules unless it explicitly converts into firm-scale/manufacturing capability.
- **C-B — More control, less scale.** Include localisation, local-content, European-preference, screening or security choices only when they produce a firm-level cost, delay, fragmentation or subscale trade-off.
- **C-C — Scale through outside capital or markets.** Require a causal link: foreign capital, markets, platforms, partners or overseas production enable European firm growth/scale while dependence remains. A foreign actor plus the generic word “investment” is insufficient.
- **C-D — Value and scale move abroad.** Broaden beyond literal firm exits to R&D/production relocation, IP/value capture abroad and acquisitions that actually move control/capability. A foreign acquisition alone is not D if the source shows the European capability remains and scale improves.

## Rules & coordination

- **R-A — Europe sets and coordinates rules.** Include common standards, mutual recognition, coordinated/joint funding or procurement, science-diplomacy frameworks and faster common decisions when the source supports an R&I performance/control gain. The existence of a programme alone is insufficient.
- **R-B — Safeguards add friction.** Actor-sensitive: this is for EU/member-state self-imposed security/autonomy safeguards with an evidenced innovation/collaboration cost. Foreign export controls are not automatically interpreted as European autonomy gains.
- **R-C — Access on outside rules.** Require a unilateral foreign rule/standard/licence/platform regime **and** evidence that compliance preserves or enables European technology/market/research access while reducing autonomy. Mere exposure to a foreign rule is insufficient.
- **R-D — Rules fragment or block Europe.** Include foreign restrictions that block European access and internal European fragmentation/delay that weakens both control and performance, but require a concrete R&I/technology consequence.

## Cross-cutting changes

- Reviewed/manual Matrix adjudications no longer bypass the semantic cell contract. Their saved source-evidence basis is treated as a strong prior, but it must still state the row mechanism and both axes.
- Cell C in every row now requires a **productive external-dependence mechanism**, not just “foreign + capacity/scale/access”.
- Cell B in every row requires a genuine **control-for-cost trade-off**, with the control-increasing action attributable to Europe/EU where relevant.
- Cell D requires a concrete loss/constraint mechanism rather than generic risk vocabulary.
- Cell A requires an observed or committed capability-building mechanism, not aspiration alone.
- Search banks were widened to cover alternative valid mechanisms without weakening admission or using cell quotas.

## Current-state re-audit

The stricter classifier drops several placements that were clearly not defensible Matrix evidence, including the Africa radio-astronomy weak signal, a US EV-leadership paper, a generic Ukraine regional-economy paper, and a Portugal productivity paper that had been forced into productive-dependence cells by broad terms.

Two talent items were also corrected from K-A to K-C because their evidence is explicitly an **external** research-talent inflow: the Nature “science superpower” item and the ERC outside-Europe applications/relocation signal.
