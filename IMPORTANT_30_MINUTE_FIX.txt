V17.2 — 30-MINUTE + REPEAT-WORK FIX

This is not the old 55-minute design and not the V17.1 "do the whole backfill in one run" design.

The GitHub job remains 30 minutes and the scanner remains capped at 20 minutes, but V17.2 additionally stores persistent source-family cursors in radar.json.

It therefore does not spend every scheduled run restarting the same large report/journal search universe.

Key values:
- .github/workflows/radar-scan.yml: timeout-minutes: 30
- radar_config.json: scan_budget_seconds: 1200
- radar_config.json: network_reserve_seconds: 150
- OpenAlex: 40 rotating queries/run
- Crossref broad: 35 rotating queries/run
- Crossref priority: 45 rotating tasks/run
- institutions: 18 rotating sources/run

The upload commit to main starts the first scan immediately. Normal schedule: every 12 hours.
