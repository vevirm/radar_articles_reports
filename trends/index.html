How to read these pairs

The two numbers always add up to 100. A 50–50 split means the opposing forces look similarly strong; 60–40 means one has a modest edge. We consider the source quality, what has actually been implemented and the strength of the contrary evidence. These are not probabilities, measurements or predictions.
