Reading the tug-of-war

Each card shows a live pull and the force pushing the other way.

Current evidence has to pull in both directions.

A one-way movement stays a developing pattern until a credible counter-pull appears.

The balance reflects the strength and independence of the evidence.

It is a tug-of-war score, not a forecast.

Older evidence can reveal roots, persistence or reversal.

It adds perspective without deciding the current balance.
