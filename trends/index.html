How a pattern gets on this page

The weighting keeps the balance lively. The admission rule stays simple.

Each side needs three current records from at least two sources.

One event or one publisher cannot make a trend.

Stronger and more independent evidence gets more pull in the balance.

The number is a tug-of-war score, not a forecast.

Older publications can show roots, persistence or reversal.

Material older than six months cannot create a current trend.
