# V17.8.2 — balanced Sovereignty Frontier

V17.8.2 fixes the second V17.8 overcorrection: V17.8.1 preserved the corpus correctly, but made the Frontier structurally hostile to openings.

## Problem

V17.8.1 required a narrow set of realised-outcome verbs for Column A and explicitly deferred A-cell gap searches whenever B/C/D remained sparse. That produced zero openings even when the corpus contained concrete EU capability-building actions.

## Fix

1. Frontier gap allocation again treats all 16 cells symmetrically by scarcity and rotating cursor.
2. Column A accepts realised gains **or concrete committed implementation** with a direct autonomy + competitiveness mechanism.
3. Pure strategies, proposals, recommendations and hypothetical “could/should/must” statements remain outside A.
4. Negative wording in an anchor paper cannot by itself veto a positive current signal; direct-signal direction takes precedence.
5. Material external partnerships remain dependency candidates when the European gain relies on a non-EU actor.
6. Automatic B/C/D ranking bonuses were removed.

On the supplied `radar (18).json`, the corrected classifier yields 19 qualifying frontier items: 3 A, 3 B, 10 C and 3 D. These counts are evidence-driven, not quotas.
