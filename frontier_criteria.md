# Matrix criteria — V17.13.28

## Reader-facing structure

The public Matrix deliberately uses **simpler language than the main Radar**. It is an analytical map, not a methodology page.

Rows:
1. **People & knowledge**
2. **Tools & facilities**
3. **Firms & growth**
4. **Rules & decisions**

Columns:
- **More control, stronger** — Europe gains control or reduces reliance and R&I capability improves.
- **More control, some cost** — Europe gains control, but the same mechanism brings a cost, delay, friction or performance penalty.
- **Stronger, but reliant** — European capability improves because of an outside input, while reliance remains.
- **Less control, weaker** — Europe loses control/access/capability and R&I performance weakens.

Cell labels are direct outcome phrases such as **Build and keep talent**, **Strength from outside**, **Useful outside access**, **European firms grow at home**, **Access on outside rules**, and **Rules split or block Europe**.

`frontier/quick/` shows the picture and short findings. `frontier/` adds source evidence; technical placement diagnostics are secondary and disclosed on demand. `priorities/` uses the same simple analytical wording.

## Internal continuity

The classifier still uses the stable analytical mapping underneath the public wording:

- `knowledge` → People & knowledge
- `infrastructure` → Tools & facilities
- `conversion` → Firms & growth
- `rules` → Rules & decisions

and quadrants A/B/C/D still mean the four combinations of independence/control and competitiveness direction. The simpler labels are a presentation layer, not a change in analytical geometry.

## Eligibility and flow from the radar

Every admitted substantive Strand-A finding is offered to the Matrix classifier. There is no separate frontend `dynamic` gate. A record may still be excluded when its evidence does not support a defensible row and two-axis direction.

For vetted evidence, classify from the source-backed summary/relevance/review evidence, not merely from the shortened display sentence.

Direction markers such as dependence, sovereign capacity, scale, cost, delay or leadership are **evidence**, not mandatory keywords. A mechanism can establish direction without the exact vocabulary.

## Claimed vs implied direction

`quadrant_claimed` records an advocated, projected or stated destination when present. `quadrant_implied` records the condition/direction supported by the document's evidence. Do not silently merge them; explicit reviewed `quadrant_implied` controls placement when supplied.

## Subject sensitivity

The direction has to apply to Europe/EU. A source saying that another actor gains talent, capability or competitiveness does not automatically tell us Europe's direction. Inference-only external-shock admission is disabled. An external development is classifiable only when the source evidence itself establishes the European R&I consequence and the row/direction mechanism.

## Weak signals

Weak signals are stricter than core evidence for Matrix use. They need an external event/mechanism capable of moving a stored phenomenon and evidence for the direction of that movement. Thematic similarity alone does not fill a cell.

## Coverage

There are no cell quotas. Sparse cells are useful evidence gaps. Current coverage is recalculated from the bundled evidence under the V17.13.26 semantic contract. Empty or sparse cells are treated as discovery gaps, never filled by weakening criteria.


## Coverage and scanner rotation

Coverage is diagnostic, not a quota. The scanner uses the current Matrix distribution to rotate extra discovery toward thin cells, using a bounded median cell count as the moving target. This reduces persistent blind spots while preserving evidence-based classification and unchanged admission rules. Rich cells can still grow through ordinary rotation; sparse cells simply receive more search attempts.


## V17.13.6 placement precision
A non-reviewed evidence record must satisfy the semantic contract of the selected row and quadrant before it appears in the Matrix. Generic AI mentions do not establish the infrastructure row. Reviewed source-level Matrix adjudications are preserved as adjudicated evidence. This rule changes classification precision, not the radar admission gate.


## V17.13.26 balanced semantic contract

All 16 cells were audited for both failure modes: excluding plausible causal mechanisms and admitting generic/irrelevant reasoning. A stored reviewed quadrant is now a strong prior rather than a bypass; its source-evidence basis must still express the row mechanism and both Matrix axes.

Key distinctions are now explicit:

- K-A is European-owned/retained talent capacity; specifically international/third-country talent that strengthens Europe while remaining externally sourced is K-C.
- K-D includes brain drain **and** source-supported knowledge leakage, exclusion from research networks or loss of know-how that harms European capability.
- I-A includes diversification/shared/secure capacity, not only literal ownership. I-C requires an external tool/input that actually enables European performance while access remains dependent.
- C-C requires outside capital/markets/platforms to causally enable scale; C-D covers value/IP/R&D/production moving abroad but not acquisition alone.
- R-B is actor-sensitive to European self-imposed safeguards; R-C is foreign-rule-dependent access; R-D covers concrete foreign blocking or European fragmentation/delay.

Across all rows, A requires an observed/committed capability mechanism, B requires a control-for-cost trade-off, C requires productive dependence, and D requires a concrete loss/constraint mechanism. Generic words such as “risk”, “capacity”, “investment”, “market” or “foreign” cannot establish a cell by themselves.


## V17.13.28 language contract

Matrix wording is simpler than main-Radar wording. Public cell names and findings should answer “what changes for Europe?” with short concrete language. Terms needed only for audit/classification — confidence, screening score, claimed/implied quadrant, semantic contract details — belong in the technical disclosure or Excel, not in the default Matrix reading path.
