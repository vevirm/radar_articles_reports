# Sovereignty-Frontier criteria — V17.8.1 risk-first

Column A is a **demonstrated opening**. It requires realised evidence of both greater European autonomy and stronger R&I/industrial performance. Strategies, proposals, roadmaps, recommendations, funding calls and ambiguous evidence do not qualify.

Neutral/underspecified evidence remains outside the 4×4 matrix instead of being forced into a positive quadrant. Discovery allocation prioritises constrained-autonomy, productive-dependence and double-loss cells while those risk cells remain sparse.

The rows remain:

- **Knowledge & people** — research talent, collaboration, science, knowledge security and mobility.
- **Infrastructure & inputs** — compute, chips, data centres, energy, materials, facilities and strategic inputs.
- **Conversion** — firms, scale-ups, production, capital, procurement and industrial/commercial capability.
- **Rules & institutions** — standards, regulation, export controls, funding, governance and decision capacity.

The columns remain:

- **A Demonstrated opening** — more independent and more competitive, with realised evidence.
- **B Costly autonomy** — more independent but less competitive.
- **C Productive dependence** — less independent but more competitive.
- **D Double loss** — less independent and less competitive.

Strand B methods are excluded from matrix evidence. The Frontier uses substantive Strand-A evidence, matrix-only recovered evidence, and Strand-C weak signals as contextual updates.
