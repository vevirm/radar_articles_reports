# Stage 7 — claim-native trends, wow and selection

Stage 7 removes the Stage-6 publication compatibility lock. It does **not** alter Scanner admission, Deep Scan decisions, the authoritative active-corpus boundary, or reader prose templates.

The live claim backend now applies the specification's post-detection gates: exact-object wow over current + historical evidence (with era/split exceptions), the oddity/stakes filter, candidate-specific falsifier execution, same-story folding, soft product targets, six-point hysteresis, movement state, reserve/watch counts, and the page-level wow-gap report. A planned query never counts as an executed falsifier: only the contiguous finding-context query prefix that the scanner records as actually executed can satisfy the denial test. A newly admitted counter-evidence claim on an executed falsifier kills the candidate in the same scan.

Trend/counter-trend pairs are claim-native. Each side must have at least three current primary records from at least two independent sources in the six-month window. Records stating both directions are excluded, near-duplicate reporting is collapsed into corroboration, source independence decays, and raw-count pull plus adjusted evidence pull become a clamped 15–85 band. Reader trend discovery now uses only the selected claim-native registry once `selection_stage >= 7`; the old hand-authored pair list remains only as a pre-Stage-7 fallback.

Stage 7 still leaves presentation wording/lint to Stage 8. Reader-facing labels and prose are not generated here.
