# Stage 5C — role-constrained shadow precision

This patch remains shadow-only. It does not switch any live detector or reader output.

What changed:

- `dependency_pathway` now enforces the R-32 role meanings instead of allowing any negative diagnosis on the frontier to fill a role.
  - propagation must bind the dependency and show spread across sites/contexts;
  - exposure must bind the capability side and state scarcity, concentration, dependence or another concrete vulnerability;
  - delivered analysis cannot by itself become a risk trigger;
  - the trigger may sit downstream on the exact-object branch (the compute worked example can therefore run energy -> permitting);
  - the risk/shock hop ceiling applies to all load-bearing roles and the trigger.
- Candidate distance continues to exclude the records actually used by the chain.
- `conflicting_criteria` now prefers a divergence on the arbitration branch, preventing a generic downstream openness diagnosis from displacing the national research-security comparison in the quantum worked example.
- The Strand-C semantic diagnostic now checks primary event claims only and no longer treats words such as `funding` inside a diagnosis as proof that the record was misclassified.
- High use of `assesses` is retained as an advisory rather than silently rewritten; explicit relational diagnoses should still be enriched when Deep Scan actually states a relation.
