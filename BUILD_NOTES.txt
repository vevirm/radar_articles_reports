V17.6.1 B METHOD-DEVELOPMENT PRECISION

- A is the substantive evidence layer: EU R&I in geopolitical/economic-security context.
- B is a method-development library for understanding the future of A. A source must explicitly develop/propose/introduce/design/adapt/extend/refine a futures method, framework, protocol or toolkit.
- Applying, comparing, evaluating, benchmarking or validating an existing method is not enough by itself.
- Generic techniques such as Delphi, system dynamics, ABM and expert elicitation require an explicit futures purpose.
- Domain-specific methods outside A require genuine generality or explicit transferability beyond the case.
- B discovery queries target new/novel/developed/adapted method contributions.
- C contains A-anchored weak signals that may change how A is interpreted.
- B is excluded from Sovereignty-Frontier evidence and matrix occupancy.
- Persistent source/query/page rotation cursors are preserved across the quality-profile bump.
- Full repository regression suite passes under the stricter semantics.
