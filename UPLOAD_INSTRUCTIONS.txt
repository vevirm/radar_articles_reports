V11 UPLOAD

1. Keep your existing populated radar.json in the GitHub repository.
2. Upload/replace the files from this package over the current repository.
3. Commit to main.
4. The push starts the V11 scan immediately.
5. Because the old radar does not have the V11 source-expansion marker, that first V11 scan searches the previous four calendar months for A/B reports and research papers, then merges them into the cumulative corpus.
6. After that, the workflow runs every 12 hours with a 14-day A/B overlap and scans the full configured source universe each time.
7. Do not add an empty radar.json from a package; this package intentionally does not contain one.
