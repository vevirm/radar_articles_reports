Everything the scanner knows about each publication

The Excel opens on one row per publication or report. It includes the current Radar plus every Historical scanner finding. Historical rows appear as soon as the Historical scanner finds them, then the same rows are updated with Deep Scan verification, admission and correction details. Authoritative historical material is clearly marked; pending or non-retained historical findings stay visible for audit.
