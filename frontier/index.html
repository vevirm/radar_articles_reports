A Sovereignty-Frontier Signal qualifies when an observed event, decision, trend break or emerging development materially changes at least one of these questions:

1 · SustainCould the EU sustain the activity without reliance on a non-EU actor?
2 · CompeteIf it did so, would it remain competitive against the best available alternative?
3 · FailWhat could cause either condition to fail?

Signals are placed into A Opening, B Costly autonomy, C Productive dependence or D Double loss, then triaged for system reach, reversibility, a policy-attention-gap proxy and actionability. Opening is intentionally strict: it requires demonstrated gains on both axes; intentions and announced funding are not counted as gains.

Attention gap is a proxy. A static repository cannot know with certainty what is already receiving mainstream EU policy attention. The score uses recency, source type and direct EU-policy linkage as a transparent proxy.
