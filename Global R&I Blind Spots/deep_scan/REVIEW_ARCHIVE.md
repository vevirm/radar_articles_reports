## OECD Main Science and Technology Indicators — R&D expenditure growth and international comparison, March 2026

**KEEP** — China / Technology leadership and industrial capacity
Package `gri-0695d5fcfe063e9670e73b70`; 2026-11-03T16:21:05.177099+00:00.

Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/data/insights/statistical-releases/2026/03/oecd-overall-rd-growth-stable-government-rd-budgets-decline-and-reorient-towards-defence.html

OECD estimates for 2024 indicate Chinese R&D expenditure was about 102% of US expenditure under the OECD’s contemporaneous purchasing-power conversion, but closer to 90–95% using a fixed 2020 PPP benchmark and about 50% at market exchange rates. China and the US are extraordinarily large research investors, yet asserting an unqualified Chinese R&D lead would overstate what the statistics show.
