## WIPO Global Innovation Index 2026 country profile and innovation indicators — South Africa

**KEEP** — South Africa / Innovation systems, infrastructure and knowledge transfer
Package `gri-0695d5fcfe063e9670e73b70`; 2026-11-03T16:21:05.177099+00:00.

Source: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-innovation-index/2026/index

South Africa was ranked 63rd globally in WIPO’s 2026 GII and identified as an innovation overperformer for a ninth consecutive year. Yet its 2024/25 national R&D survey reports a 1.9% real decline in R&D expenditure and an R&D-intensity fall from 0.62% to 0.60% of GDP, with weaker business investment. The combination suggests sustained innovation strengths operating under deteriorating research-investment conditions.

## WIPO Global Innovation Index 2026 country profile and innovation indicators — Singapore

**KEEP** — Singapore / Innovation systems, infrastructure and knowledge transfer
Package `gri-0695d5fcfe063e9670e73b70`; 2026-11-03T16:21:05.177099+00:00.

Source: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-innovation-index/2026/index

Singapore held fifth place in WIPO’s 2026 GII, ranking first for innovation inputs but ninth for innovation outputs. Its government maintains an explicit multiyear research–enterprise policy architecture through RIE2030, while Singapore’s statistical office reports 2023 R&D spending of S$13.75bn (2.03% of GDP, versus 1.81% previously). The combination shows a high-capacity, deliberately coordinated innovation ecosystem with measurable conversion challenges.

## OECD Main Science and Technology Indicators — R&D expenditure growth and international comparison, March 2026

**KEEP** — China / Technology leadership and industrial capacity
Package `gri-0695d5fcfe063e9670e73b70`; 2026-11-03T16:21:05.177099+00:00.

Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/data/insights/statistical-releases/2026/03/oecd-overall-rd-growth-stable-government-rd-budgets-decline-and-reorient-towards-defence.html

OECD estimates for 2024 indicate Chinese R&D expenditure was about 102% of US expenditure under the OECD’s contemporaneous purchasing-power conversion, but closer to 90–95% using a fixed 2020 PPP benchmark and about 50% at market exchange rates. China and the US are extraordinarily large research investors, yet asserting an unqualified Chinese R&D lead would overstate what the statistics show.

## OECD Main Science and Technology Indicators — international R&D comparison 2026

**KEEP** — China / Research funding, institutions and talent
Package `gri-0695d5fcfe063e9670e73b70`; 2026-11-03T16:21:05.177099+00:00.

Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/main-science-and-technology-indicators.html

China’s reported R&D intensity reached approximately 2.7% of GDP in 2024, matching the OECD-area average and exceeding the EU’s roughly 2.1%; Chinese national data subsequently report 2.80% in 2025, with basic research accounting for 7.21% of R&D expenditure. This is a sustained change in research-system scale and mix, not a claim that China leads in all research quality or commercial outcomes.
