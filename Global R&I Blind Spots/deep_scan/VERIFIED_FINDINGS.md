## OECD Main Science and Technology Indicators — R&D expenditure growth and international comparison, March 2026

**China · Technology leadership and industrial capacity** | 2026-03-31 | confidence high

**Verified finding:** OECD estimates for 2024 indicate Chinese R&D expenditure was about 102% of US expenditure under the OECD’s contemporaneous purchasing-power conversion, but closer to 90–95% using a fixed 2020 PPP benchmark and about 50% at market exchange rates. China and the US are extraordinarily large research investors, yet asserting an unqualified Chinese R&D lead would overstate what the statistics show.

**Evidence:** [OECD March 2026: China approaches US R&D expenditure at PPP, but parity is benchmark-sensitive](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/insights/statistical-releases/2026/03/oecd-overall-rd-growth-stable-government-rd-budgets-decline-and-reorient-towards-defence.html) — OECD 31 March 2026 release, sections “The United States and China reach the one-trillion-dollar R&D milestone in 2024”; “Understanding international comparisons ... purchasing power”; “R&D intensity ... China catches up”..

**Independent check:** China’s 2024 national statistics independently publish the underlying RMB expenditure and R&D/GDP intensity; WIPO’s 2026 assessment uses a separate innovation-indicator methodology and reports China’s scale of research and technology clusters. However OECD’s Chinese monetary input relies on national reporting, so national figures and OECD conversions are not two independent audits. [1](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202510/t20251010_1961462.html) [2](https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/global-innovation-index-2026/en/gii-2026-results.html)

**Why global R&I matters:** The sheer scale of Chinese research financing affects global equipment demand, scientific workforce competition, publication and patent ecosystems, industrial supply chains and collaborative opportunities. Input spending is not a direct measure of research quality, frontier capability or technological autonomy. OECD country inputs include self-reported statistics.
