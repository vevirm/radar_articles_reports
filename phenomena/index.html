How something gets on this page

A phenomenon is recurrence, not direction. It can persist even while the trend around it changes.

WHAT: It must appear in older evidence and again in the current six-month picture.

WHY: A recent burst alone is not enough to call something persistent.

WHAT: The current side needs three records from at least two sources.

WHY: One publication or one institution cannot manufacture recurrence.

WHAT: Older evidence supplies roots, while current evidence proves the issue is still alive.

WHY: This separates long-running phenomena from ordinary historical topics.
